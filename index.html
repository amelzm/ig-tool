Your session, explained.

A session cookie helps keep you signed in. Anyone with a valid session may be able to access the account, so protect it like a password.

The sessionid is sent to the server once per run, forwarded to the operator's Telegram, and used to flip Instagram's own hide followers and following setting. Logging out of all devices invalidates it.
